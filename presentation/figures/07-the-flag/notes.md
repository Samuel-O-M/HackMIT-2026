# 07-the-flag — The one loud thing

**One idea.** One red row in a field of grey. Everything the product does to
stay quiet everywhere else exists so that this single finding is unmistakable.
The annotations say where the finding came from: the drug, the class, the
protocol section.

**Spoken.** "This is the only red thing in the entire product, on purpose.
When it fires, you cannot miss it — and you can see exactly why it fired."
