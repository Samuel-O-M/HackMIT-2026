# 04b-time — the time it saves

**One idea.** Manual pre-visit reconciliation costs the coordinator hours; with
the agent placing the calls, they only review a finished draft. Same caseload,
same scale, hours back.

**Spoken.** "Thirty participants, twelve minutes each by hand — that is six
hours. With the calls made for you, it is ninety minutes of reviewing. Four and
a half hours back, every round."

**Numbers.** Illustrative, not measured: 30 participants × 12 min manual vs
30 × 3 min review.
