# 04c-doctor-vs-agent — before, and after

**One idea.** The same visit, split. **Before**, plain and slow: the coordinator
reads the record, calls and holds it all in mind, then types it into a computer
hours later — clocks show each step costs 30, 45, 60 minutes. **After**,
branded and live: the Reconmed mark, the record loaded before the call and
updating *as the call happens* — 3 minutes to review, nothing to write up.

**Spoken.** "Before: half an hour reading, three quarters of an hour on the
phone holding it all in your head, then an hour typing it into the computer
afterwards. After: the record's already loaded, and it updates as the call
happens. Three minutes to review. Four and a half hours back, every round."

**Visuals.** Clocks drawn with hands; a monitor for the manual write-up; the
Reconmed mark on a lit green panel with live change chips.
